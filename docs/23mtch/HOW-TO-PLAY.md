# Stickler: how to play

Sticks lie on a table. You and Sparky take turns picking some up, and **whoever takes the last stick loses**. Pick a mode in the Game menu to start a round: in Classic 23 there are 23 sticks, you may take 1, 2 or 3 at a time, and you move first. There is a trick that wins every time. Sparky knows it, and the Coach will teach it to you if you ask.

## Controls

| Key | Mouse or touch | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `1`–`9` | Click or tap sticks | Pick sticks. A number key picks that many at once; a click picks one, and clicking it again puts it back. On tables with rows, picks come from the chosen row |
| `Enter` or `Space` | **Strike** button | Take the sticks you picked |
| | **Take 1**, **Take 2**, … | Take that many at once. One-row tables only; each button shows its number key |
| | **Sweep** button | Take the whole pile when a Handfuls sweep is due |
| `↑` `↓` | **Row** buttons | Choose a row on tables with several rows. Your pick is cleared |
| `H` | **Hint** button | Ask the Coach. Each press goes one step further. Not in Versus |
| `R` | **Rematch** | On the results card: the same table again (a new surprise pile in Handfuls). On the Daily the button says **Play again (practice)** |
| | **Next table** | On the results card, after a Ladder win |
| | **Share result** | On the results card, after a Daily |

In **1981 mode** you type a number and press `Enter`, or use the **1**, **2**, **3** and **4?** buttons under the screen. `Esc` still pauses while you are typing.

The rest follows the collection's navigation standard (`docs/NAVIGATION.md`): `Esc` or the **Pause** button pauses and resumes a round; on the results card `Esc` goes to the Game menu and `H` goes back to the Hall (during play, `H` asks for a hint); in the Game menu `Esc` goes back to the Hall and `K` opens the Keyring; `?` lists the shortcuts anywhere. Every control in the Game menu is a button you can reach with `Tab`.

## The rules

1. **The table.** Sticks lie in one row, or in a few rows on some Ladder tables. The chip under the title says what this table allows, for example "23 sticks · take 1–3" or "rows 1-3-5 · take any from one row".
2. **Your turn.** Take at least one stick and no more than the limit. On a table with rows, take as many as you like, but all from one row.
3. **The last stick loses.** Whoever takes it sets it off and loses. Sparky never takes it if he has a choice; when it is the only stick left on his turn, he has to.
4. **Breaking the rules.** Pick too many, too few, or from two rows and Sparky objects. Nothing else happens: pick again.
5. **Who opens.** You do, except on Ladder tables 4 and 10 and in Handfuls with **Sparky starts**. In Versus, Player 1 opens.
6. **Every table can be won.** Whatever table you meet against Sparky, perfect play from your first move wins it, even against Master and even when he opens.

### Handfuls

Handfuls adds a floor to every take. With a range of 3–5 you must take at least 3 and at most 5. When fewer sticks are left than the floor, or exactly the floor, the only move is to **sweep** them all at once, and that takes the last stick, so whoever has to sweep loses. You may also take the whole pile whenever it fits the range, and that loses just the same. The line under the pile shows the range and turns into a warning when a sweep is due. The take buttons offer only the legal range, and when a sweep is due a single **Sweep** button replaces them and clicking any stick picks the whole pile.

## Modes

### Classic 23

The original game: 23 sticks, take 1 to 3, you start. Sparky plays at the level you chose in the Game menu. Start here, or press the **Play Classic 23** button on the matchbox.

### Handfuls

One row where every take is a real handful. Choose a range: 2–3 (23 sticks), 2–4 (27), 3–4 (32), 3–5 (36) or 4–6 (45). Two switches change the deal:

- **Surprise pile** deals a random pile between 3 and 6 times the sum of the range (15 to 30 sticks for 2–3, 30 to 60 for 4–6), always one you can win. A rematch deals a new one.
- **Sparky starts** gives him the first move from a pile one stick shorter (22, 26, 31, 35 or 44). He opens on a losing count, so answer him correctly and you win.

Sparky plays at your chosen level. The Game menu remembers your last setup and shows your wins on each range, with a crown once you have beaten Master on it.

### Ladder

Ten tables, each unlocked by clearing the one before. The rules and Sparky's level are fixed per table.

| # | Table | Rules | Sparky | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up | 11 sticks, take 1–3 | Rookie | |
| 2 | Getting warmer | 15 sticks, take 1–3 | Rookie | |
| 3 | The Classic | 23 sticks, take 1–3 | Clever | |
| 4 | Sparky starts | 21 sticks, take 1–3 | Clever | Sparky opens |
| 5 | Take up to four | 27 sticks, take 1–4 | Clever | |
| 6 | Take up to five | 34 sticks, take 1–5 | Master | |
| 7 | Bonfire | 45 sticks, take 1–6 | Master | |
| 8 | Two rows | rows of 3 and 5 | Clever | Any number from one row |
| 9 | Three rows | rows of 1, 3 and 5 | Master | Any number from one row |
| 10 | Marienbad | rows of 1, 3, 5 and 7 | Master | Sparky opens |

Each win earns up to three stars (see Scoring, below). After a win, **Next table** takes you straight on.

### Daily

One puzzle a day, the same for everyone, against Master Sparky. The Daily section below has the details.

### Versus

Two players on one screen, passing the keyboard or the mouse between turns. Pick a table: Classic 23, Quick 15, Big 31 (take 1–4), Marienbad 1-3-5-7, Handfuls 2–3 with 23 sticks, or Handfuls 3–5 with 36. There is no Sparky and no Coach; a card at the top says whose move it is and shows a short reminder when a pick breaks the rules. Versus games do not change your wins, losses or streak.

### 1981 mode

The original program as it behaved on an IBM PC, on a green screen. You always start with 23 sticks. Type how many you take and press `Enter`. Anything other than 1, 2 or 3, or more than what is left, gets a complaint and another try; letters get BASIC's own `?Redo from start`. When the computer is losing it always takes exactly 1, and when you leave it one stick it gives up. A new game starts straight away. The words on the screen are new; the behaviour is the original's. 1981 mode does not count towards your record, but winning once earns a keycap.

## Settings

In the **Game menu**:

- **Sparky's level**: Rookie (half his moves are guesses), Clever (knows the trick, sometimes forgets) or Master (never misses a win). It applies to Classic 23 and Handfuls; the Ladder and the Daily set their own.
- **Stick style**: Matches, Glow sticks or Candy sticks. The style changes the sticks, the table, Sparky's colours, the sounds and how the last stick goes up. Matches and Candy sticks have a light and a dark table; Glow sticks keep their dark table in both themes.
- **Handfuls setup**: the range and the two switches, in the Handfuls panel.
- **Forget my data**, under your record, wipes Stickler's progress and keycaps from this browser after asking first.

In the **Settings** sheet (pause menu, or the Keyring page): sound on or off, volume, appearance (Light, Dark or System) and motion (Follow my device, Less motion, Full motion). Less motion turns off the table's shake and cuts the flights, flares and bursts short. The top bar also has a mute button and an appearance button.

## Scoring

There are no points. A round ends in **You win!** or **Burnt!**, and the results card says who took the last stick, or who had to sweep and how many.

- **Your moves** appear as a row of squares, one per move: green kept a win, red gave the win away, grey means no move could have won from there.
- **The key moment** explains the first red square: how many were left, what you took, and what would have kept you winning.
- **Ladder stars**: 3 for a win without a hint, 2 if you used the Coach's first or second step, 1 if you needed the third (the exact move). Your best on each table is kept, out of 30 in all.
- **Your record** in the Game menu counts wins, losses and your best winning streak across Classic 23, Handfuls, the Ladder and the Daily, plus your Handfuls wins on each range. Everything is saved in this browser only.

## Daily

- A new puzzle arrives at your own midnight. Everyone who plays on the same date gets the same table.
- Dailies are numbered from 1 January 2026, which was #1; 7 October 2026 is #280. The Hall and the Keyring show today's number too.
- The table is one row of 17 to 42 sticks with a limit of 3, 4 or 5 per take. You always open, Sparky plays at Master, and the table can always be won.
- Your first finished game of the day is the one that counts: it is kept with the date, it adds a day to your Keyring daily streak, and a win counts towards the **Daily Habit** keycap. After that the mode card says whether you solved it, and replays are practice (they still count as wins or losses in your record).
- **Share result** puts four lines on your clipboard, or opens the share sheet on a touch screen: "Stickler Daily" with the number, the table ("22 sticks · take 1–3"), a row of coloured squares for your moves followed by "won" or "burnt" and how far you went with the Coach ("no hints", or "1 hint" up to "3 hints"), and the link to the game.

## Tips

1. **Watch the count after Sparky's turn.** In Classic, whenever he can, he leaves you 21, 17, 13, 9, 5 and finally 1. Those are the numbers to leave him.
2. **From 23, take 2. Then take 4 minus whatever he took.** Every round removes exactly 4 sticks, and he ends up with the last one.
3. **New limit, new number.** With takes of 1 to *k*, leave him one more than a multiple of *k* + 1: with take up to 4 that is 1, 6, 11, 16 and so on.
4. **Handfuls has zones, not numbers.** With a range of *a* to *b*, the counts to leave him are the bands 1 to *a*, then the same band again every *a* + *b* sticks (for 3–5: 1–3, 9–11, 17–19…). Answer each of his takes with (*a* + *b*) minus it. Land at the top of a band and he will have to sweep the whole floor at the end: that is how Clean Sweep is won, on a range that starts at 3 or 4.
5. **Rows: think in 4, 2 and 1.** Split every row into fours, twos and ones, and leave each of them an even number of times across the table. When every row is down to one stick or none, leave an odd number of single sticks.
6. **When Sparky opens, let him.** Ladder 4, Marienbad and Sparky starts all hand him a losing count. Don't panic; answer him.
7. **Hints are cheap on the Ladder.** The Coach's first two steps still leave you two stars; only the third, which shows the exact move, drops you to one.
