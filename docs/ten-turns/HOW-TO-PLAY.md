# Ten Turns in the Pit: how to play

Last ten turns in the pit without the rabbit landing on you, and a rope ladder comes down. Choose how to play on the
game menu (Classic or Tactics), then press Play.

## Controls

| Key | Mouse or touch | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Q W E` `A D` `Z X C` | A pad round your character | Step one cell in that direction (the letters follow your keyboard: see Settings) |
| Number pad `1`–`9` | | The same eight directions |
| Arrows | | Step; two arrows together step diagonally |
| Move a cursor with the keys above, then `Enter` | Click in the pit | Choose where to be dropped |
| `1` `2` `3`, then a cell (`Enter` or click) | The tool tray, then a cell | Toss a carrot, set a stone, dig in (Tactics) |
| `Esc` | | Put a chosen tool back |
| Hold `I` | | Show the rabbit's plan, when Settings has it on request (Tactics) |
| `Space` | Click | Hurry the rabbit's pounces along |
| `Esc` | Pause button | Pause |
| `R` / `H` | Buttons | On the results card: play again / back to the Hall |

A refused step (into the wall, a rock, a stone or a rabbit) makes the cell shake and says why underneath.

## The rules

Both play styles share the pit and the rabbit:

- The pit is a circle of radius 10 (12 in the campaign's last chapter) on a grid of cells.
- **The drop.** You start by choosing where to land. Land right on the rabbit and it is squashed flat: you win at
  once.
- **Your turn.** Take one step to any of the eight cells around you.
- **The rabbit's turn.** It pounces one cell (one across and one up on a diagonal) towards its target. If it misses,
  it may pounce again: one chance in 2 after the first pounce, then 1 in 3, then 1 in 4, and so on. The pips in the
  top corner show the odds and light up as the rabbit rolls.
- **A landing** on your cell is a crunch, and the game is over. You can't step onto the rabbit yourself.
- **Ten turns** (more in some pits) and you are released.

### Classic

The 1970s game put right. You don't know where the rabbit is when you choose your drop; after you land it is placed
somewhere you can still survive with good play (at least 40 % of the time), or, one time in 317, right under you.
Every pounce goes for the cell you are on now. No tools, no hat.

### Tactics

A board game you can read:

- **The plan.** A dotted arc with a red ring shows where the rabbit's **first** pounce will land. That pounce is
  locked on the cell you stand on when your turn begins, so stepping away from it is the first thing to do. Any
  extra pounces chase wherever you went.
- **The hat.** Your bobble hat saves you once: the rabbit snatches it instead of you and springs back.
- **Rocks and stones** stop a pounce dead. The rabbit bonks, its turn ends, and on its next turn it is dazed: it
  hops round obstacles instead of into them.
- **Burrows.** Step into one to hide for a turn: the rabbit circles it, sniffing. It caves in when you climb out.
  Two burrows joined by a tunnel (the same coloured ribbon) take you in at one end and up at the other.
- **Rustling grass.** Before the drop, a patch of grass rustles where the rabbit is hiding. Drop on it for a chance
  at a squish, or keep well away.

**Tools** (some pits give you some, the tray shows how many):

| Tool | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Carrot | `1` | Toss it up to three cells away. Every pounce goes for it, and a rabbit that reaches it eats it and stops for the turn. Pull more from carrot patches next to you. |
| Stone | `2` | Set it on a cell next to you. Pounces bonk on it. |
| Spade | `3` | Dig a burrow where you stand and hide in it for a turn. |

Using a tool or pulling a carrot takes your turn instead of a step. If rocks and stones box you in completely, a pad
under your feet (or `Enter`) lets you stay put for the turn.

**The rabbits.** Each pit's note says what you are up against:

| Rabbit | How it pounces |
|---|---|
| Plain | One cell a pounce; another pounce 1 in 2, then 1 in 3, 1 in 4… |
| Jumper | Keeps its odds of pouncing again for longer: 1 in 2, 1 in 2, 1 in 3, 1 in 3… |
| Stalker | Covers two cells in one pounce, but never pounces twice |
| Clever | Guesses where you will step and locks its first pounce there; a jumper's legs |

## Modes

| Mode | What |
|---|---|
| Classic | The 1970s game, put right |
| Tactics | Twenty pits in five chapters: The Pit, Burrows, Carrots, Two Rabbits, The Big One. Each pit opens when you survive the one before. |
| Daily Pit | One Tactics pit a day, the same for everyone |
| 1970s mode | The original program on a terminal: type where to be dropped, then an angle to run at (0 is east, 90 north). Every quirk is still there. |

## Settings

Game settings (from the game menu or the pause menu):

- **Play style**: Classic or Tactics.
- **Look**: the Spooky Storybook. Three more styles are coming.
- **The rabbit's plan** (Tactics): always shown, only while you hold `I`, or never.
- **Keyboard**: QWERTY, AZERTY or QWERTZ, so the eight letters form the same square on yours.
- **Animation speed**: relaxed, normal or brisk.
- **Motion**: as your device, reduced (no camera moves, slow motion or shaking) or full.
- **Sound**: on or off. The top bar's sound button mutes everything.
- **Forget my data**: clears this game's records and keycaps; your collection-wide settings stay.

## Scoring

You either climb out or you don't. Tactics pits also give three medals:

- **Survived**: you were released, or squashed the rabbit at the drop.
- **Tools to spare**: you survived with at least one tool unused (or the pit had none to use).
- **Untouched**: you survived with your hat still on.

Records keep Classic's games, releases, squishes, crunches and best run, every pit's best medals, your Daily Pits and
your games on the terminal.

## Daily

A new Tactics pit every day at midnight, your time: its ground, its tools and its rabbit come from the day's number,
the same for everyone. Before it is offered, a search checks it is fair: from the best drop, with no tools, good play
survives between 70 % and 92 % of the time. Try it as often as you like. The share button
copies a line with your turns, tools and medals, no spoilers; it always describes your first try of the day.

## Tips

- Step **off** the cell you start your turn on. In Tactics the first pounce is aimed there.
- Watch the pips before you move: a rabbit with good odds of pouncing again wants room between you.
- The pit's wall is your enemy as much as the rabbit: near it you have fewer ways to run.
- A carrot is worth a whole turn. Toss it so the rabbit has to go away from you to get it.
- A stone between you and the rabbit, on the line it will pounce along, is a turn in the bank, and the next turn
  the rabbit has to go round it.
- In Classic the drop matters less than you would think, because the rabbit is placed fairly after it. Halfway out
  on a diagonal is a shade better than the very middle: about 60 % with perfect play, against 55 %.
