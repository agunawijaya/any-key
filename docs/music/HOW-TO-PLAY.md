# Bop Box: how to play

Bop Box is a music box, not a race: there is nothing to win and no way to lose. Pick one of eleven tunes from a 1981 PC, pick one of twelve styles to play it in, and press play. The studio opens on The Blue Danube, played by an orchestra. Press **Space** to hear it. On your first visit the instruments take a moment to download ("Tuning the band…" with a percentage); the PC Speaker ’81 style needs no download and plays at once.

## Controls

| Key | Mouse or touch | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Space | The round play button | Play and pause. When a button has keyboard focus, Space presses that button instead |
| A to K | A tune in the *Tunes* list | Pick a tune. The letters are the ones the 1981 menu used, in either case |
| 1 to 9, 0, =, - | A style card | Pick a style (see *Modes*). The music keeps its place in the tune |
| L | *1981* or *Full*, above the view | Switch between the 1981 excerpt and the whole piece, keeping your place |
| V | The view tabs | Next view: Sheet music, Falling notes, Numbers, Stage |
| ← → | The position bar | One bar back or forward |
| Home, on the position bar | The back-to-start button | Back to the first beat |
| | The loop button | Play the tune round and round |
| | The speed menu | Half speed to one and a half times (0.5× to 1.5×) |
| | A player on the Stage, or a name under *Band* | Mute that player; press again to bring them back |
| | The piano keys | Play a note yourself |
| | *MIDI* or *WAV* | Download what you are hearing |
| Esc | *Back to the Hall* | Back to the Hall. If the Story tab or a sheet is open, Esc closes that first |
| ? | | The list of shortcuts |

Bop Box has no rounds, so it has no Pause button and no pause menu: the play button is the pause. **K** picks Scales here, as it did in 1981, so open the Keyring with the button in the top bar. The Story tab holds this manual and five more pages about the game and the program it came from.

## The rules

There are no rules to break, only things to try, roughly in the order you will meet them:

1. **Play.** Press Space. The view scrolls with the music, and every key on the piano lights up in the colour of the instrument playing it.
2. **Change the style.** Press a number (or click a style card) while the music plays. The new band picks up at the same bar.
3. **Change the length.** Every tune comes in two lengths. *1981* is the excerpt the 1981 program played; *Full* is the whole piece, from about a minute (Yankee Doodle) to nearly fourteen minutes (The Blue Danube in the Orchestra style). The times under the two buttons are for the style you have picked. Press **L** in the middle of the 1981 excerpt and the full piece carries on from the same note. Outside that stretch, switching back to 1981 starts the excerpt from its top.
4. **Change the tune.** A new tune starts from its beginning, and every muted player comes back.
5. **Listen to the end.** When a tune finishes, the music stops, unless Loop is on.

The **Original Score** style is special. For three tunes (Gounod's march, Sousa's march and Mozart's symphony) it plays every part the composer wrote. The first time you pick it for one of those, the orchestra fills in for a moment while the score opens ("opening the score…"). For the other eight tunes it plays our own setting for the instruments the tune belongs to, and says so.

## Modes

### Styles

| Key | Style | What you hear |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orchestra | Strings, winds, brass and timpani, in the tune's own meter and tempo |
| 2 | Pop | Piano, synths and a punchy kit |
| 3 | Jazz | Swing, a walking bass and a sax on top |
| 4 | House | 124 BPM, four on the floor |
| 5 | Dangdut | Kendang drums, suling flute and mandolin |
| 6 | Koplo | Dangdut, but faster and wilder |
| 7 | Lo-fi | Dusty beats to study to, with vinyl crackle |
| = | Hip-hop | Trap: 808 slides, hi-hat rolls, a flute on the tune |
| 8 | Solo | Just the tune, on the instrument you choose from the menu that appears (18 to choose from) |
| 9 | PC Speaker ’81 | The beep of the 1981 speaker: its slightly sharp pitch, its clipped notes and its 26-key piano on a blue screen. Notes that fall off that piano light an "off the edge" sign |
| 0 | AI Remix | A different genre for every tune, arranged by Claude: phonk, cumbia, chiptune, trance and more |
| - | Original Score | The composer's own orchestration, or our setting where none survives |

### Views

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Sheet music | The tune and its chords on a staff that slides under a fixed line; the note on the line is the one you hear |
| Falling notes | Every player's notes falling onto the keyboard, like a piano tutorial video |
| Numbers | *Not angka*, the numbered notation taught in Indonesian schools: 1 is *do*, dots above or below change the octave, underlines halve a note and each dash holds it a beat longer |
| Stage | The whole band, each player lighting up as they play and showing the note they are on |

## Settings

Bop Box has no pause menu, so its settings live in three places:

- **The top bar:** sound on or off, and the appearance (Light, Dark, or System to follow your device).
- **The Keyring page, under Settings:** the same sound switch and appearance, the volume, and motion (Follow my device, Less motion or Full motion). Less motion stops the colour glides between styles; the score and the falling notes still move, because they are the music's own timing.
- **The studio itself:** the length (remembered in this browser for your next visit), the speed, Loop, the Solo instrument and the mutes (these last four reset when you leave).

## Scoring

Bop Box keeps no score: there are no points, stars or times. The keycaps below are the only thing it collects.

## Achievements

| Keycap | Tier | How to earn it |
|---|---|---|
| First Note | Common | Press play on any tune |
| Beep Beep | Common | Play a tune in the PC Speaker ’81 style |
| Band Leader | Common | Mute a player in the Band or on the Stage, then bring them back |
| Take It Home | Common | Download an arrangement with the MIDI button |
| Whole Jukebox | Rare | Play each of the eleven tunes, in any style and length |
| Style Hopper | Rare | Play something in each of the twelve styles. Switching style while the music plays counts |
| The Long Version | Rare | Hear a full-length piece through to its last note, with Loop off |
| Concert Hall | Legendary | Hear all eleven full-length pieces through to their last notes |

Bop Box has no secret keycaps. On the Keyring a common keycap is worth 10 XP, a rare one 30 and a legendary one 80.

## Tips

1. On your first visit, press **9** while the band is still tuning: the 1981 speaker needs no download.
2. Play the Funeral March (A) in PC Speaker ’81, then press **1** halfway through. Same bar, a whole orchestra.
3. In PC Speaker ’81, watch the ends of the little keyboard during the Funeral March: its lowest notes fall off the 26 keys the 1981 screen had.
4. Mute the tune's own player in the Band, and play the melody yourself on the piano keys.
5. To learn a tune, pick the Numbers view, set the speed to 0.5× and turn on Loop.
6. For Style Hopper, give the Original Score a moment after you pick it: until the score has opened, the orchestra standing in counts as Orchestra.
7. *MIDI* opens in any notation app or music program, where you can see every part of the arrangement, not just the tune.
