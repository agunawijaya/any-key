# Two Humps: how to play

Get your camel forty miles across the Gobi to the ger camp before your pursuer catches you, before your water
runs out, and before the camel gives up. Pick **Crossing** on the game menu, choose who is behind you and how
hard, and set off. New here? The one-minute **Tutorial** walks you through a walk, a drink, a gallop, the reins
and a camp.

## Controls

| Key | Mouse or touch | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `1` | Drink card | Take a sip from the canteen |
| `2` | Walk card | Walk on |
| `3` | Gallop card | Gallop |
| `4` | Make camp card | Rest, or sleep till dawn |
| `5` | Scan card | Climb a dune and look ahead |
| `6` | Signal card | Signal for help |
| `Space` | Pull the reins button, or click the camel | Stop the camel while it runs on |
| `Enter` | Let it run button | Let the camel take one more stride; also skips ahead through a moment |
| `S` | Seeds button, or drag the sack onto the gang | Toss a handful of seeds to the Ladle Gang |
| `C` / `W` | Chase them / Wait it out | Answer a dust devil |
| `P` | Pause button | Pause |
| `R` / `H` | Buttons on the journal page | Play again / back to the Hall |

`Esc` pauses and opens the menu, as in every Any Key game. In 1982 mode you type numbers and press `Enter`, or
press `F1` to `F6`.

## The rules

### A turn

Each command except Drink and Scan is a turn, and a turn is two hours on the trek's clock. Before you choose,
every card shows what it will do now: the miles a stride can cover, how tired the camel will be after it, and how
far your pursuer can come meanwhile. Nothing is hidden but the dice.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Drink** | One sip: your thirst goes back to four turns. Takes no time. With an empty canteen, it can't be done |
| **Walk** | 0 to 9 miles, and the camel tires by one hump |
| **Gallop** | 0 to 18 miles in steps of two, and the camel tires by three humps |
| **Make camp** | The camel is fresh again. By day it's a two-hour rest; after dark you sleep until dawn, and every two hours of the night counts as a turn for your pursuer |
| **Scan** | Costs a sip (it doesn't quench your thirst). Shows any oasis within 15 miles, and the odds of each event on this stretch. Takes no time |
| **Signal** | Flash a mirror by day or wave a torch by night. One time in ten (15% on Chill, 7% on Hard) herders see you and bring three sips and a rest for the camel. A turn passes either way |

Night falls at 18:00 and the sun is up at 06:00. In the dark every stride covers 40% less ground.

### Water, thirst and the camel

- The canteen holds **six sips** (seven on Chill, five on Hard). Each turn uses up one turn of thirst; after four
  turns without a drink you're warned, and one turn later the trek is over.
- The camel has **seven humps** of strength, shown at the top. A move it can't afford is refused, and the card
  says why.
- The humps sag as the camel tires. That's true of real Bactrian camels, whose humps are fat stores.

### The camel runs on

After a walk or a gallop, if nothing else happens on the way, the camel may keep going by itself. A dial shows the
chance that it carries on (58% on Normal when the stretch is quiet) and what one more stride costs.

- Every stride on the run covers the same range as the command and tires the camel the same amount.
- Strides on the run use **no water and no time**, and your pursuer doesn't move.
- **Let it run** (`Enter`) to take the stride, or **pull the reins** (`Space`, or click the camel) to stop. The
  turn then ends.
- If a stride would take the camel past seven humps, it lies down, and the trek is over. The dial warns you first.

### Things that happen

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Oasis** | The camel smells water and stops there. The canteen is filled, your thirst is reset, and the camel recovers two humps |
| **Gust** | The wind shoves you up to six miles forward or back |
| **Sore hump** | Your next stride covers only half the ground |
| **Dust devil** | Your packs are scattered. **Chase** them: half the time it's fine, half the time it costs the camel two humps. Or **wait**: always safe, but your pursuer gets a free step |
| **Singing dunes** | A low hum rolls off a dune and the camel perks up: two extra miles on the next stride |
| **Fossil** | Only at Bayanzag, in the Expedition: something pale in the sandstone goes into your journal |

Any of these ends the camel's run. Dunes sing more often on dune stretches, and the camel gets sore more often on
gravel.

### Pursuers

| | Where it starts | How it moves | Its trick |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Haboob** | 11 miles behind | 2.4 to 5.4 miles a turn by day, 1.4 to 3.2 at night | It never stops. It catches you if it reaches you |
| **The rival caravan** | Beside you at dawn; camped 8 miles ahead at dusk | 2.5 to 5.8 by day, 7 to 12 in the first turn after dawn, nothing at night | It drinks every oasis it reaches first, and they're dry when you get there. It wins if it reaches the ger camp first. A tall dust plume means it may gallop |
| **The Ladle Gang** | In its burrows at your start (8 miles back at dusk) | Sets off after five turns (four on Hard); 0 to 1 mile by day, wild hops of 2.5 to 11 at night | It catches you if it reaches you. A handful of seeds stops it for a turn; you have three |

On Chill pursuers move 15% slower, and on Hard 15% faster.

### How a trek ends

You win when the camel reaches the ger camp. You lose if your pursuer catches you (or, for the rival, gets there
first), if you go five turns without a drink, or if the camel runs past its strength. Nobody gets hurt either way.

## Modes

| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Crossing** | Forty miles. Pick a pursuer and a difficulty. The trek sets off at dawn in the light appearance and at dusk in the dark one |
| **Expedition** | Five legs of 30 miles through real places, west to east: Khermen Tsav (no galloping in the canyons), Khongoryn Els (the dunes sing often), Bayanzag (fossils), Yolyn Am (an ice gorge: no thirst, but strides 15% shorter) and a ger camp (herders nearby answer four signals in ten). Each leg has its own pursuer and hour, and ends on a page of field journal. Win a leg to open the next; you can walk the ones you've done again |
| **Daily Crossing** | One crossing a day, the same for everyone: Normal difficulty, setting off at dawn, with the pursuer taking turns day by day. Your first try is recorded and can be shared; after that it's practice |
| **1982 mode** | The original program's rules in a terminal: see below |
| **Tutorial** | A short crossing against the Haboob on Chill, with a coach. Skip it whenever you like |

### 1982 mode

Type a number and press `Enter`, or press `F1` to `F6` or click the keys along the bottom of the screen: 1 drink,
2 walk, 3 gallop, 4 rest, 5 status, 6 help. The pursuers are the Ladle Gang, and every line on the screen is new,
but the numbers are the 1982 program's, and so are its bugs:

- The camel sometimes repeats a move without telling you: each repeat costs strength, and only the last stride's
  miles count.
- Asking for your status uses up a drink.
- Any number other than 1 to 6 counts as hoping for help, which works one time in ten.
- After an ambush, `8` (wait) is always safe; anything else is a run for it, which fails half the time.
- At the end, only a capital `Y` starts another trek. Type `RUN` to start again after the program stops.

A panel under the screen says what BASIC doesn't show: the hidden repeats, where the gang really is, and the
return addresses piling up on BASIC's stack. The dice fall the same way every time you open 1982 mode, as they did
in 1982.

## Settings

**Records and settings**, on the game menu, holds Two Humps' own settings:

- **Difficulty** for Crossings and the Expedition: Chill, Normal or Hard (canteen size, pursuer speed, how often the
  camel runs on, how often help comes).
- **Pursuer** the Crossing card starts on.
- **Distances** in miles or kilometres.
- **Forget my data**: clears Two Humps' records and keycaps from this browser.

Sound, light or dark and reduced motion are the collection's, in the top bar and in Settings on the pause menu.
With reduced motion the scenery holds still and each turn fades in instead of sliding.

## Scoring

There are no points. A trek is won or lost, and the journal page shows how many turns it took, how far you got,
the sips left and your lead. Records keeps wins, plays and your fewest turns for each pursuer at each difficulty.

## Daily

The Daily Crossing changes at midnight, local time. Everyone gets the same oases, dice and pursuer. When you finish
your first try, Share copies three lines like these, with a small emoji picture where the brackets are:

```
Two Humps · Daily #12 · The Ladle Gang
[camel][camel][drop][camel][camel][tree][camel][moon][camel][flag]  40 mi in 11 turns
Gang 3 mi behind · 2 sips left
```

One picture per turn: a camel for a move, a drop for a drink, a tree for an oasis, a moon for a night's sleep, a
tent for a rest by day, a note for singing dunes, a sparkle for help, and a flag at the finish (or what stopped you, if it didn't go well).

## Tips

1. Drink when the thirst bar turns red, not before: a sip is worth most when you're about to need it.
2. Gallop on a fresh camel, walk on a tired one, and pull the reins with a hump or two to spare.
3. Against the Haboob, rest in the day instead of sleeping through the night: a night's sleep gives it six turns.
4. Against the rival, sleep while he sleeps, and race him to the oases at dawn.
5. Against the Ladle Gang, cover ground by day while they sleep, and keep a handful of seeds for the night.
6. Scan once early: knowing where the next oasis is tells you how long your water has to last.
